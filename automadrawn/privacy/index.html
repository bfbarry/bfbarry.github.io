Swing Swag Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Swing Swag does not collect, store, or share any personal data. All processing (e.g. audio analysis) happens locally on your device, and nothing is sent to us or to any third party.

Crash reports

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your Mac's settings, Apple may provide us with anonymized crash reports. These are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Data retention and deletion

We don't hold any of your data, so there is nothing to retain or delete. Uninstalling the app removes all of its local data.

Changes

If this changes, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date.
