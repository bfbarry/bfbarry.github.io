---
title: "Vicarious Vice"
year: "2026"
date: 2026-08-05
slug: "vicarious-vice"
---
I sit at home
yet live a thousand lives

A bath of media overload
Buzzing like a hive

For my ear, more love songs
Feelings on streaming

In my eyes, more fun
On my mind: nothing

You can douse the flame
daily

Or reject the same
and flee
