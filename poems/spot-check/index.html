Spot Check

There’s nothing like a good cruise
No, not the boat!

The act of driving through
Checking which zone is firing the most

Sometimes there’s slower days
That’s when I spark the J

Windows down, blasting reggae
A mellow stoke permeates

I park by the spot
Waves in front like a drive in

Right on the dot
A set wave and its kin

The menu is bigger when solo
Myriad options to observe

Still doesn’t beat a crew though
On a psychedelic treadmill, sharing the surf
